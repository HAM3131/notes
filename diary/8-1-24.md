I wanted to start writing diary entries more regularly a few weeks ago, but I'll start today because some interesting things happened and I want to take some notes.

At Northrop Grumman, I and the others in the Pictoria building finally moved into the main building with the rest of the Splatypus team! This is exciting, and it'll be very nice for the next few weeks of my internship--which ends on August 23rd--to be around people in person for collaboration and learning. In the middle of the day, around 11:30am, I went to lunch at Rooster's with Brett Starr, my boss at CIC in 2023. I wanted to speak with him about a possible job, and things went much better than I expected!

We sat down and first started talking for a bit about family and sport. Finally, we got to some business related talk. I told him about my internship experience at NG, and he told me about some of the projects he has been working on at CIC. When I told him I am planning on taking time away from school and want a full time job, he seemed excited to hook me up with a role he's been looking to fill! Apparently there is an "Application System Admin" role he has been looking to fill, and he literally texted the hiring person about it as we were sitting. When we left the restaurant, he used voice-to-text on his phone to say "I found our guy." So, I'm feeling pretty good about it. I gave him my email and as I am sitting here typing this I have an email with a link to apply to the job posting!

Please note - this is not an IT infrastructure role.

In the application there are some questions, so I will include my responses and any research I do here.

### Salary
> What is your salary expectation for this role?

A cursory google search found that for an Application System Admin [the average pay is $93,995](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Salaries/Application-Systems-Administrator-Salary) in Cincinnati, with the 25th percentile being $77,500. With this in mind, and with the job page listing an annual salary of $55,000-$85,000, I am going to list a value in the middle. I should likely make below the 25th percentile considering I don't have a degree, but I can easily negotiate down if necessary and it would be great if I can start at a solid $70,000.
