## No Tax on Tips
This dumbass proposal was Donald Trump's idea which I believe he first called for in July, 2024. Soon after, in early August, Kamala Harris took ownership of the proposal too. Likely with the intention of allowing Trump no breathing room on worker's issues. And that's not ridiculous. The proposal *sounds* nice on it's face, and to low income workers who make the majority of their income in tips, it's very appealing.

However, the proposal is really a loser. It provides a tax break for an arbitrary subset of the population for seemingly no reason--why should a service worker paid in tips making $50,000 a year pay less in taxes than any worker making the same amount salaried? It encourages companies to shift the cost of wages onto the consumer, while creating a needless barrier to eliminating the tipped minimum wage. Not to mention the fact that most tipped workers already hardly pay any federal income tax.
