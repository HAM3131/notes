## "anti-Zionism is Antisemitic"
No, it's not.
* [Debunking the myth that anti-Zionism is antisemitic](https://www.theguardian.com/news/2019/mar/07/debunking-myth-that-anti-zionism-is-antisemitic) - The Guardian piece by Peter Beinart
  * This piece is great. It presents a step-by-step argument on why it is clearly wrong that anti-Zionism is antisemitic based on combatting three flawed premises:
    * "That opposing Zionism is antisemitic because it denies to Jews what every other people enjoys: a state of its own."
    * "Maybe it is not bigoted to oppose a people’s quest for statehood. But it is bigoted to take away that statehood once achieved."
    *  "As a practical matter, the two animosities simply go together."

## 2024 Campus Protests
2024 saw a series of protests spread throughout college campuses all around America. These protesters constructed encampments where they would protest Israel's actions, primarily calling for BDS: Boycott, Divestment, and Sanctions. These demonstrators wanted to get their colleges to divest from Israel.

These protests saw extreme reactions from both media and administrations, but the reality of the protests was far more tame than it was portrayed, while actual violence often came from police or counter-protestors.
* [What’s Really Happening on College Campuses, According to Student Journalists](https://www.politico.com/news/magazine/2024/05/03/college-campus-protests-israel-gaza-student-journalists-00155672) - Politico piece by Catherine Kim
  * This piece offers a great variety of perspectives from student journalists who were on the ground at campus protests around the country.
