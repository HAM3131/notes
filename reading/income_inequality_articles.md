## Wage Theft
Wage theft is a real problem, and when you put into perspective the magnitude of wealth that is stolen from working class people with the relatively minor impact of shoplifting and burglary, it's hard not to see the real criminals: corporate America.

* [An Epidemic of Wage Theft Is Costing Workers Hundreds of Millions of Dollars a Year](https://www.epi.org/publication/epidemic-wage-theft-costing-workers-hundreds/) - Economic Policy Institute piece by Brady Meixell and Ross Eisenbrey
